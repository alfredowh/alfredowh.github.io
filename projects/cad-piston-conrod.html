Kolben & Pleuel — Piston and Connecting Rod

HS Hannover · B.Eng. Mechatronics · CAD Design Project (1st Semester) · Winter 2017/2018

  • Autodesk Inventor
  • Parametric 3D Modeling
  • Assembly Design
  • Technical Drawing

Overview

One of my first hands-on CAD projects in the B.Eng. Mechatronics program at HS Hannover: a parametric 3D model of a piston and connecting rod (Pleuel) assembly, designed in Autodesk Inventor in the 1st semester.

Design & Assembly

  • Modeled the piston (ring grooves, wrist-pin bore, skirt) and a two-piece connecting rod — rod and cap — split at the big-end bore.
  • Assembled the parts with mate/insert constraints and standard bolts, nuts, and retaining rings; produced 2D drawings and a STEP export.
